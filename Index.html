How a project moves

The same four stages, whether it's a mow-and-go or a full backyard rebuild.

  1. Walk the property

    We come out, look at the ground, the sun, the drainage, and talk through what you actually want from the space.

  2. Send a written estimate

    Itemized, no vague line items — you'll know what you're paying for before anyone touches a shovel.

  3. Do the work

    Our own crew, our own equipment, scheduled around the weather rather than in spite of it.

  4. Keep it up

    Most clients move into a recurring maintenance plan once the initial project wraps.
